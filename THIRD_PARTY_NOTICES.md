## Feedback sounds

The files `start.wav`, `stop.wav`, `cancel.wav`, and `error.wav`
in `Resources/Sounds/` are the "soft" sound set of Hex
(https://github.com/cheetahbyte/hex, `resources/audio/sounds/soft/`, as of
commit 9d6e0990), unmodified. Hex generates them with
`scripts/sounds/synth.py` from sine waves; they contain no recordings or
samples from other sources.
